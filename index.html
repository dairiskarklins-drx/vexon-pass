The problem

Every upgrade disappears with the receipt.

Exhaust, protection, electronics, servicing — years of investment go into a bike. But by the time it's for sale, the receipts are long gone, the service book is worn out, and the buyer has to take your word for it. Vexon Pass logs every purchase the moment it happens, so the value doesn't vanish along with the paper.

Usually the first thing lost

Exhaust receipt · Service sheet · Tyre invoice · Protection kit packaging · Electronics warranty

How it works

Three steps. No paper.

01

Snap a photo

Take a picture of a receipt, invoice, or parts packaging — straight from your phone, mid-ride or in the garage.

02

The system reads it

Item, amount, date and seller are logged automatically. Check the details and fix anything that needs it.

03

Pass it on

When you sell the bike, send one link. The next owner sees the full history and continues the same chain.

What the buyer sees

History that can't be faked overnight.

Every entry carries a timestamp that can't be changed. Records that are naturally spread across years are a more honest proof than any stamped service book ever was.
